Can statutory regulatory audits be accelerated from months to weeks without compromising precision?

For the past several months, our team has been architecting the Autonomous AI Truing-Up Analytical Tool for the Kerala State Electricity Regulatory Commission (KSERC). By orchestrating high-fidelity PDF table extraction with multi-provider LLM fallback pipelines on Google Cloud Run, we reduced the tariff petition audit cycle from 6-12 months down to 2-4 weeks.

Deploying AI into statutory environments requires rigorous verification over generative speculation. Every metric must trace back to the verified petition schedule.

Grateful to KSERC officials and RIET mentors for the statutory trust and guidance.
