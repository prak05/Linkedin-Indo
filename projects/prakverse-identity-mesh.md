# PrakVerse: Local-First Digital Identity & Career Asset Mesh

**Domain:** Personal Knowledge Management (PKM), Local AI, Career Asset Automation  
**Repository:** [https://github.com/prak05/PrakVerse](https://github.com/prak05/PrakVerse)  

---

## 📌 Executive Overview
Developers and engineering leaders maintain fragmented profiles across GitHub, LinkedIn, personal websites, and resume iterations. Inconsistent claims, broken links, and forgotten metrics reduce credibility during technical recruiting and partnership evaluations.

**PrakVerse** is a local-first single-source-of-truth knowledge mesh and portfolio engine that synchronizes project specifications, verified career achievements, academic credentials, and interactive web showcases with deterministic reproducibility.

---

## ⚡ Technical Architecture & Engineering Stack
- **Knowledge Core:** Python, SQLite FTS5 (BM25), Sublinear TF-IDF Vector Cosine Engine.
- **Frontend & Visual Showcase:** Modern responsive HTML5/Tailwind interface with Perplexity dark/light theme options, interactive project matrices, and animated HUD components.
- **Verification Engine:** Automated cross-checking against local source documents, academic seminar transcripts, and official certificates.

---

## 📊 Key Highlights & Synchronization
- **Single-Source-of-Truth:** All resumes, LinkedIn drafts, and portfolio matrices originate from one immutable verified dataset.
- **Zero Hallucination:** Directly powers LinkedIn Indo's RAG facts injection and Perplexity Knowledge Base queries.
