# CardioPredict AI Diagnostic Suite: Predictive Cardiology & Heart Failure Risk

**Domain:** Healthcare AI, Clinical Decision Support, Biometric Analytics  
**Repository:** [https://github.com/prak05/MEDICAL_AI](https://github.com/prak05/MEDICAL_AI)  

---

## 📌 Executive Overview
Cardiovascular disease represents the leading cause of global mortality. Early stratification of high-risk patients via routine clinical parameters enables proactive therapeutic interventions and reduces ICU readmissions.

**CardioPredict AI** is a predictive cardiology diagnostic platform that evaluates multimodal clinical telemetry, echocardiographic markers, and blood biochemistry to predict heart failure decompensation risks.

---

## ⚡ Technical Architecture & Clinical Stack
- **Algorithms:** Gradient-boosted decision trees, ensemble voting classifiers, and regularized logistic risk scores.
- **Data Engineering:** Automated missing-value imputation, outlier trimming via Tukey's fences, and feature normalization.
- **Tooling:** Python, NumPy, Pandas, Scikit-Learn, Seaborn / Matplotlib.

---

## 📊 Key Highlights & Clinical Metrics
- **Multi-Factor Risk Stratification:** Analyzes ejection fraction, serum creatinine, platelets, and systolic pressure.
- **High Sensitivity Baseline:** Optimized for clinical recall (>92%) to minimize false-negative misclassifications in triage.
- **Interactive Risk Dashboard:** Clear probability breakdowns for clinical rounds and patient counseling.
