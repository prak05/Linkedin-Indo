## 📌 Executive Overview
Contract review and statutory compliance verification for enterprise agreements (NDAs, MSAs, vendor contracts, employment agreements) is labor-intensive and error-prone. Critical indemnification clauses, governing law conflicts, and liability caps are frequently missed during rapid business growth.

**Yugality** is an enterprise AI contract intelligence platform that parses complex multi-party legal agreements, extracts obligations, identifies non-standard indemnification clauses, and maps them against Indian statutory laws and industry standards.

---

## ⚡ Technical Architecture & Engineering Stack
- **Backend & Legal Ingestion:** Python, FastAPI, PyMuPDF, LangChain, PostgreSQL (with pgvector for clause indexing).
- **Frontend & Workflows:** Next.js, React, Tailwind CSS, TypeScript.
- **AI Extraction Engine:** Hybrid retrieval pipeline extracting contract clauses, risk ratings, and redline suggestions with citation traceability.
